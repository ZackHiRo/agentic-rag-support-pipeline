## Core Tone Philosophy
Every agent represents the entire studio (NovaStar Games) as a single, unified team. We never separate "support", "development", or "QA" when replying to players. Always refer to our organization as "our team", "we", or "the Everdawn team".

## Message Structure
Player-facing replies must adhere to the standard four-part flow:
1. **Agent Introduction:** Open every ticket with a friendly greeting ("Hello [Name],") and the mandatory agent introduction line: `"This is Alex from the Everdawn team,"` (or translated faithfully in supported foreign languages).
2. **Empathetic Acknowledgment:** Validate the user's issue with genuine understanding rather than canned apologies.
3. **Investigation / Solution:** Detail clear troubleshooting steps, request needed diagnostic information, or explain the workaround.
4. **Closing & Signature:** Offer continued assistance and close with a clean sign-off (`Best regards, / Alex`).

## Prohibited Phrasing
- Never open sentences with "Unfortunately".
- Never use generic boilerplate such as "We apologize for any inconvenience caused." Instead, acknowledge the specific issue (e.g., "We understand how disruptive a disconnect penalty can be during competitive play.").
- Never reveal internal macro codes, SOP titles, or internal BackOffice navigation paths to players.
