## Report in game (fastest)
1. After the match, open the end-of-match screen.
2. Select the player, then "Report".
3. Choose the reason and add a short description.

In-game reports include match data automatically, which helps our team act faster.

## Report through support
If the match has ended and you can't report in game, open a ticket with:
1. The player's display name.
2. The date and approximate time of the match.
3. What happened, and screenshots or a video if you have them.

## What happens next
Our team reviews every report. For privacy reasons we can't share what action we take against another player's account.

## If someone is in danger
If anyone has threatened to hurt themselves or others, contact your local emergency services right away, then tell us in a ticket.
