## Before you start
Check the Everdawn server status page and our social channels. If there is an ongoing outage or scheduled maintenance, there is nothing to fix on your side: matches will recover once service is back.

## Quick fixes
1. Use a wired Ethernet connection instead of Wi-Fi if you can.
2. Restart your router: unplug it for 30 seconds, then plug it back in.
3. Restart the game and your PC or console.
4. Close downloads, streams and other apps that use your connection.

## On PC
1. Flush your DNS: open Command Prompt and run `ipconfig /flushdns`.
2. Allow Everdawn through your firewall and antivirus.
3. Verify your game files in your launcher (Steam: right-click Everdawn > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity).

## Still disconnecting?
Open a ticket and attach your network log so we can look at what happened. On PC, the file is `NetLog.txt` in `%localappdata%/Everdawn/Saved/Logs`. Tell us your platform, your server region, and roughly when the disconnects happened.

## Disconnect penalties
If a disconnect was caused by a server problem on our side, we can review the penalty. See "Disconnect penalties explained".
