## What's happening
Some parties with players on different platforms (for example PC and console) see `Matchmaking Error 105` when they enter the queue. This is a known issue and our team is working on a fix.

## Workaround
1. The **party leader** opens Settings > Gameplay > Matchmaking Region.
2. Change the region from "Auto" to the server closest to you.
3. Disband the party, then re-form it.
4. Enter the matchmaking queue again.

Make sure everyone is on the latest game version (v2.4.1 or higher).

## Still seeing Error 105?
If the error continues after you set a manual region and restart your device, open a ticket. Tell us your platform, your party's platforms, and attach a screenshot of the error.
