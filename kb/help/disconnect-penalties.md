## Why penalties exist
Leaving a match early ruins it for everyone else, so disconnecting gives you a short matchmaking cooldown. The cooldown grows if it happens several times in a short period.

## Server-side crashes
If you were disconnected because of a server crash on our side, open a ticket. Tell us your platform, your region, and roughly when it happened (date, time and time zone). We'll check our server records for that match and remove the penalty if a server issue caused it.

## Connection problems on your side
Penalties caused by your own connection can't be removed, but the fixes in "Fix disconnects, lag and timeouts" can stop it from happening again.
