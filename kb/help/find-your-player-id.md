## What it is
Your NovaStar ID identifies your Everdawn account across every platform. It looks like `EVD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`. Support asks for it so we can find your account quickly.

## Where to find it
1. Launch Everdawn and open the main menu.
2. Go to Settings > Account.
3. Your NovaStar ID is shown under your display name. Select "Copy" to copy it.

You can also find it on the NovaStar website after signing in, under Profile.

## Keep it safe
Your NovaStar ID is safe to share with Everdawn support. Never share your password or the verification codes we send you. Support will never ask for them.
