## Reset your password
Choose "Forgot password" on the NovaStar sign-in page. We'll email you a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

## Lost access to your email
If you can no longer use the email on your account, we can verify ownership another way. Open a ticket and include:
1. Your NovaStar ID (see "Find your NovaStar ID").
2. Proof of your **earliest** purchase in Everdawn: the receipt from Steam, PlayStation Store or Xbox showing the transaction ID, date, and item.

Without valid proof of purchase we can't verify ownership, so we can't change the email on the account.

## Link a platform account
1. Sign in at the NovaStar website.
2. Go to Profile > Linked Accounts.
3. Choose your platform and follow the sign-in steps.

Your progress follows your NovaStar ID, so linking lets you play with the same progress on every platform.
